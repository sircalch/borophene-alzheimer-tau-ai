# Borophene $\beta_{12}$ Nanoplatelet Carrier: Source Provenance and Structural Audit

## 1. Crystallographic Origin and Literature Reference
- **Experimental Discovery & Lattice Model**: Mannix et al., *Science* 350, 1513–1516 (2015) (DOI: [10.1126/science.aad1080](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aad1080)); Feng et al., *Nature Chemistry* 8, 563–568 (2016) (DOI: [10.1038/nchem.2491](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchem.2491)); Zhang et al., *Chem. Soc. Rev.* 46, 6720–6749 (2017) (DOI: [10.1039/c7cs00261k](https://doi.org/10.1039/c7cs00261k)).
- **Lattice Constants**: Rectangular primitive unit cell with $a = 5.08\ \text{Å}$ (along the hollow row direction) and $b = 2.93\ \text{Å}$ (along the close-packed boron chains).
- **Vacancy Density ($\eta$)**: The $\beta_{12}$ borophene allotrope possesses a characteristic periodic 1/6 hexagonal hole pattern ($\eta = 1/6 = 0.1667$), distinguishing it strictly from the $\chi_3$ phase ($\eta = 1/5 = 0.2000$) and the pristine triangular lattice ($\eta = 0$).

## 2. Unit Cell Fractional Coordinates ($a = 5.08\ \text{Å}, b = 2.93\ \text{Å}$)
The rectangular primitive cell contains 5 Boron atoms and 1 vacancy across 6 triangular sites:
- **B1**: $(0.0000 \cdot a,\ 0.0000 \cdot b,\ 0.0000)$
- **B2**: $(0.5000 \cdot a,\ 0.0000 \cdot b,\ 0.0000)$
- **B3**: $(0.0000 \cdot a,\ 0.3333 \cdot b,\ 0.0000)$
- **B4**: $(0.5000 \cdot a,\ 0.6667 \cdot b,\ 0.0000)$
- **B5**: $(0.0000 \cdot a,\ 0.6667 \cdot b,\ 0.0000)$
- **Vacancy Site**: $(0.5000 \cdot a,\ 0.3333 \cdot b,\ 0.0000)$

## 3. Supercell Construction and Perimeter Passivation Rule
1. **2D Supercell**: A $3 \times 3$ supercell comprising 9 primitive unit cells is generated:
   - Total available lattice sites = $9 \times 6 = 54$ sites.
   - Total hollow vacancy sites = 9 vacancies ($\eta = 9/54 = 1/6 = 0.1667$).
   - Total constituent Boron atoms = $9 \times 5 = 45$ Boron atoms ($N_{\text{B}} = 45$).
2. **Boundary Identification**: Boron atoms situated at the finite cluster perimeter are identified by undercoordination ($\text{Coordination Number} < 4$ using an interatomic distance threshold $d_{\text{B-B}} \le 1.95\ \text{Å}$).
3. **Hydrogen Edge Passivation**: Each perimeter Boron atom is passivated by 1 Hydrogen atom placed at a canonical covalent distance ($d_{\text{B-H}} = 1.19\ \text{Å}$) along the outward normal vector opposite to its nearest boron neighbors ($N_{\text{H}} = 18$).
4. **Physical Stoichiometry**: The resulting passivated cluster has the physical formula $\text{B}_{45}\text{H}_{18}$ ($N_{\text{total}} = 63$ atoms), strictly maintaining the 1/6 hollow vacancy density of the parent $\beta_{12}$ lattice.
